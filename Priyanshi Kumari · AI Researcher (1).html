Preprints and under review

Manuscripts currently under peer review.

QFT-Net: A quantum-inspired, fractal and topological deep learning framework for pulmonary nodule characterization

Under review at Quantum Machine Intelligence

Combines a quantum-inspired encoder, fractal attention, topological persistence pooling and a chaos-theoretic uncertainty module, with concept bottlenecks and a defer-to-radiologist safety filter. On 192 LIDC-IDRI nodules it reaches a cross-validated AUC-ROC of 0.946 ± 0.030, and the topological β1 feature differs clearly between benign and malignant nodules.

Under review

An open-source interactive processor and dual-branch fusion model for Sentinel-2 land-suitability mapping

Under review at ACIIDS

Presents sentinel2-processor, a pip-installable package and desktop GUI for turning Sentinel-2 products into composites and indices, plus a CNN and tabular fusion model that scores land suitability. On a coastal Odisha tile it shows cloud cover, not vegetation, drives the score.
